> Living document. Captures the kickoff interview (2026-07-02) with Fatih and the
> design implications derived from it. Every major decision in this project should
> trace back to a line in this file. Update it when answers change.

## The one-sentence mandate

Rebuild guelerdev.de as a **full cinematic, 3D-driven experience** — radical
rethink allowed, nothing visually sacred — that makes a skeptical German KMU
owner think *"this person clearly knows what they're doing"* within one scroll,
while simultaneously fixing the real bottleneck: **near-zero traffic and zero
credible proof**.

## Derived design implications (the reasoning layer)

### 1. The core engineering thesis: cinematic AND green
"Full cinematic experience" and "Lighthouse ≥95 / CWV green" were BOTH chosen.
These are only compatible through discipline:
- Server-rendered, semantic content under every canvas — the story must exist in
  HTML before a single shader compiles. WebGL is enhancement, never the carrier
  of meaning.
- Aggressive lazy/progressive loading: three.js chunks load after LCP, scenes
  initialize on approach (IntersectionObserver), degrade by device class.
- Every scene has a designed static/reduced-motion fallback that is *itself*
  beautiful — not an empty div.
- Frame budget enforced on a throttled mid-tier mobile profile, not desktop.

### 2. Proof without testimonials
With placeholders removed and only showcase work available, credibility must come
from: (a) the craft of the site itself, (b) **honest** case-study framing
("Konzept"/showcase clearly labeled — honesty itself is a trust signal for this
audience), (c) process transparency (how Fatih works, fixed prices, timelines),
(d) a real portrait (proposed — design reserves the slot), (e) verifiable
technical proof (live Lighthouse scores of client sites, the site's own metrics).
**Never invent proof. Remove every fabricated quote and metric in `lib/content.ts`.**

### 3. Sie-form rewrite
Every string in `lib/content.ts` and all pages moves to formal address. Tone per
PRODUCT.md: direct, competent, human — Sie-form must NOT drift into stiff
Beamtendeutsch. Banned words remain banned (professionell, erfahren,
leidenschaftlich as self-praise).

### 4. Traffic workstream is first-class
Near-zero traffic means SEO/GEO work has equal priority with design: Ratgeber
content quality, structured data correctness, indexing hygiene, page speed as a
ranking factor, and analytics so results become measurable. A jaw-dropping site
nobody sees fails the mission.

### 5. Palette is open but not directionless
"Fully open" is permission to explore, not an obligation to change. The
exploration must be evidence-led (what signals premium+trustworthy to German
KMU owners) and end in ONE committed system, documented here before rollout.

### 6. Broad funnel, one voice
"Mix — don't narrow" means the site must speak to a Handwerker with a €3k budget
and a 50-employee company evaluating a €20k AI project without alienating
either. Resolution: one confident voice, offer architecture (Pakete) does the
segmentation instead of the messaging.
